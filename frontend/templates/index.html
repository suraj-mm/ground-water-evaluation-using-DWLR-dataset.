Well Catchment Hydrological Method

According to Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) Water Level Fluctuation (WLF) framework, dynamic recharge for this station is governed by:

Recharge (ham) = Area (ha) × Δh (m) × Sy
Recharge (MCM) = Area (sq km) × Δh (m) × Sy

Specific Yield Context: Represents the ratio of the volume of water which a saturated rock or soil will yield by gravity to its total volume.

This calculation quantifies the annual extractable buffer without triggering progressive water table decline in this aquifer zone.
